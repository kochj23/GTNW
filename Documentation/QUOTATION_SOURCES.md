# Quotation sources

Every epigraph in GTNW (3.20.0+), by shelf. Lines are quoted exactly. Scripture is from public-domain
translations; doctrine lines are verbatim sentences from public-domain U.S. military publications (most read from
the Internet Archive and service doctrine sites); science-fiction lines are short attributed quotations.
