# Cabinet portrait credits

GTNW's cabinet portraits (`Shared/Assets.xcassets/Portraits`, 3.19.0) are derived from photographs and paintings
that are in the **public domain** (or CC0) on Wikimedia Commons, most of them official U.S. government portraits or works
published before 1930. Each was cropped to the face, its background removed and re-rendered in a 256-colour SVGA style by
`tools/cabinet_portraits` (see its README). The originals and their authors:
