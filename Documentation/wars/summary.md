# GTNW war dataset: summary

Generated by `build.py` from `raw/` and `curated_1789_1815.json`: **803 wars**, 1786-2025. Durations are in months from the first to the last date of the war (gaps between phases included; unknown days/months taken as mid-month/mid-year). Ongoing wars are left out of duration statistics (right-censored). Format: median [25th-75th percentile] (n).

Coverage: curated 1789-1815; COW inter-state and extra-state 1816-2007, intra-state 1816-2014, non-state 1816-2007; UCDP/PRIO ACD 26.1 adds war-level episodes (1,000+ battle deaths in a calendar year) from 2008 to 2025 that COW lacks, and gives the end and outcome of wars COW left ongoing.

## Data notes

- UCDP ACD 26.1 has 628 conflict episodes (1946-2025), 178 of them war-level. War-level episodes starting 2008 or later with no overlapping COW war are added as records; minor (25-999 deaths a year) episodes are not. 10 war-level UCDP episodes from 1946-2007 have no COW match and are not added (COW's coverage; most differ in coding rules or dates): Bolivia: Popular Revolutionary Movement (1946-1946); Cameroon: UPC (1960-1961); Chad: CDR (1986-1987); China vs Taiwan (1949-1950); France vs Tunisia (1961-1961); Hyderabad vs India (1948-1948); Hyderabad: CPI (1947-1948); Indonesia: OPM (1981-1981); Israel: Hezbollah (2006-2006); Lebanon: LAA, NSF (1975-1976).
- Curated 1789-1815 wars that ran past 1816 overlap COW records of their later phases (see `related`).
- `battle_deaths` follows each source: COW battle deaths (inter-state: sum over participants; extra-state: state plus non-state deaths; intra-state: TotalBDeaths), UCDP best estimates (BRD 26.1, 1989 onward only; earlier UCDP years are missing). `deaths_approx` is true for curated values and for sums with missing parts.
